About Us

Queens Of Change Foundation is a non-profit organization working towards women empowerment, education, community support, and social awareness. Our goal is to create opportunities for women through education, support, and community programs. We believe every woman deserves opportunities, confidence, and a brighter future.

Our Mission

Education

Helping young girls and women get better education opportunities.

Healthcare

Creating awareness about health, hygiene, and wellness.

Women Empowerment

Supporting women with skills, confidence, and independence.
